FAQ

Questions you might have

Do you store my salary or savings?

No. There's no login, and your raw salary and savings figures are not stored. Savings is optional and only asked as a rough band.

Will it tell me how much I should give my family?

Never. That's your call. The Yes Check only shows whether a specific ask fits your budget over the coming months.

Is this financial advice?

No. ParivaarPlan does not give investment, loan, insurance, tax or legal advice. It's a planning aid for family money conversations.

Can I type the ask in Hinglish?

Yes. Type it the way it was said, in English or Hinglish. The message for home can come back in the same style.

Is the AI doing the maths?

No. Plain code does all the calculations. AI only reads what home asked for and writes the WhatsApp message.
